# OmniPi launch film design

## Style Prompt
Polished editorial product film based on the actual OmniPi UI. Wide navy fields, crisp white interface cards, gold π and quiet blue interactions.

## Colors
- Navy background: #101b34
- Gold symbol: #d9bd83
- Blue action: #4568d9
- White panel: #ffffff
- Pale canvas: #f7f8fc

## Typography
System sans for dependable local rendering; Georgia for π.

## What NOT to Do
No generic particle field, fake blockchain numbers without a source label, financial claims, tiny captions, or violent transitions.
